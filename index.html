Mechanical Engineering at the University of Cincinnati

Hey! I'm Sameer, a mechanical engineering student at the University of Cincinnati with a minor in mathematics. I work in UC's Gas Dynamics and Propulsion Laboratory, where I build instrumentation for rotating detonation engine testing: low-cost strain measurement systems, thermocouple signal conditioning, and Python pipelines for analyzing high-speed test data.

Outside the lab, I'm on the Cincinnati Aerospace Society's liquid propulsion team and a student engineer with the UC Rocketry Club. I was also Mechanical Design Lead for a NASA L'Space proposal team, and I'm co-developing an autonomous multipurpose UAV with Yash Kakade. I previously worked as a Manufacturing & Quality Intern at Guerbet.
